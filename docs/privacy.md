# Privacy policy: Flip and Fry

Last updated 19 November 2026. Flip and Fry is a game that runs inside Reddit posts. It is made by hatif03 (the author). This page says what the game keeps and why.

## What the game stores

When you play, the game stores the following on Reddit's Devvit platform (its Redis database for this app). Nothing is stored anywhere else, and the game makes no requests to outside servers.

- **Your Reddit user id and username**, to tell players apart, to show your name on a door, a visit note or a "neighbor at the table" tag, and to save your restaurant to your account.
- **Your restaurant**: layout, staff, menu, prices, cash, research, and the list of actions you took during each night.
- **Results**: stars, profit, awards, your streak, and which day you last played.
- **Neighborhood activity**: whether your door is open for visits, a short automatic note about how a visit to your kitchen went (no typed text), and that you had a post open recently so neighbors can be shown at the tables.
- **Test Kitchen scores** you earn in the arcade mode.
- **Reminder choice**, only if you switch reminders on.

## How long it is kept

Most neighborhood data expires on its own: presence after 2 days, open doors after 7 days, bulletin notes after 30 days, hosted-service sessions after a short time. Your restaurant and results stay until the app is removed from the subreddit or you ask for them to be deleted.

## What the game does on your behalf

It posts a comment, sets your flair, follows the community, posts your kitchen as a post, or sends a reminder **only when you press the matching button**, after it shows you what it will do. Nothing is required to keep playing. Until Reddit approves the app, those actions may run from the app's own account instead of yours.

## What it does not do

No advertising, no analytics beyond Devvit's own, no selling or sharing of data, no cookies of its own, no payments, no location, no contact lists. Upvotes, karma and other Reddit data are never read to decide how the game plays.

## Your choices

Switch your door or reminders off in the game at any time. To have your data deleted, message the author through Reddit (u/ho1505) or open an issue at https://github.com/hatif03/flip-and-fry and say which Reddit username to remove.

Reddit's own privacy policy applies to everything you do on Reddit, including this game.
